FAQ

Questions

Something else? Just ask.

Do I need a big following to work with you?

No. We work with accounts starting from zero and accounts that are already established — the plan just changes based on where you start.

Which platforms do you work on?

Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, LinkedIn, and X. We’ll help you pick the ones worth your time based on who you want to reach.

Do you run my accounts or coach me?

Both. Some clients want hands-on help creating content; others want a clear plan and coaching so they can run it themselves. We’ll recommend the right fit for you.

How fast will I see results?

Every account is different, and anyone promising overnight growth isn’t being straight with you. What we promise is a clear strategy, consistent execution, and honest reporting.

What do I get with the free audit?

A 3–5 minute video where we walk through your Instagram page and give you 3 quick wins you can act on right away. It’s free, with no obligation.

Is there a contract?

Monthly packages have a 3-month minimum, because real results take time to build. After that, your plan continues month to month.

How do I manage or cancel my plan?

Sign in to the client portal to see your plan, update your card, download invoices, or cancel. Cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing period once the 3-month minimum is complete.

Is ad spend included in Premium?

Ad management is included; the ad budget itself is paid separately, directly to the platform. We’ll recommend a budget that fits your goals.

Can the Growth Plan count toward a monthly package?

Yes. If you sign up for a monthly package within 30 days of buying the Growth Plan, the full $497 is credited toward your first month.
