FAQ

Good questions.

Don’t see yours? Just ask.

Do I need a big following to work with you?

Not at all. We work with people starting from zero and accounts that are already established — the plan just looks different depending on where you start.

Which platforms do you work on?

Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, LinkedIn, and X. We’ll help you decide which are worth your time based on who you’re trying to reach.

Do you run my accounts, or teach me?

Both. Some clients want hands-on help creating content; others want consulting and a clear plan they can run themselves. We’ll recommend the right fit on your consult.

How soon will I see results?

Every account is different, and anyone who guarantees overnight growth isn’t being honest. What we promise is a clear strategy, consistent execution, and honest reporting.

What does the free consult include?

A conversation about your goals, a look at your current accounts, and honest feedback on your biggest opportunities — no pressure to sign up.
