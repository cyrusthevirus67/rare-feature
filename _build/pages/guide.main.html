01

The 3-part video

Every video that performs does three jobs, in this order. Miss one and people scroll.

Hook0–2 seconds

Say or show the most interesting thing first. No intros, no “hey guys”. Give them a reason to stay.

HoldThe middle

Deliver the value fast. Cut every pause. Change the shot, angle or on-screen text every 2–3 seconds.

PayoffThe last 3 seconds

Land the point, then tell them one thing to do: follow, comment, save, or tap the link.

02

25 fill-in-the-blank hooks

Swap the blanks for your topic. Put the hook in the first sentence and on screen as text.

Curiosity

  1. Nobody talks about this part of ___.
  2. I tried ___ for 30 days. Here’s what happened.
  3. This is why your ___ isn’t working.
  4. The ___ mistake almost everyone makes.
  5. I wish someone told me this before I started ___.

Teaching

  1. How to ___ in under 60 seconds.
  2. 3 ___ tips I’d give my younger self.
  3. Steal my exact ___ process.
  4. Stop doing ___. Do this instead.
  5. The easiest way to ___ (even if you’re a beginner).

Proof

  1. Here’s what ___ looks like behind the scenes.
  2. Watch me ___ from start to finish.
  3. Before and after: ___.
  4. What a customer asked us this week about ___.
  5. This is what $___ of ___ actually gets you.

Opinion

  1. Unpopular opinion: ___.
  2. ___ is overrated. Here’s why.
  3. If you’re a ___, you need to hear this.
  4. I’ll probably get hate for this, but ___.
  5. Everyone says ___. They’re wrong.

Story

  1. The day I almost quit ___.
  2. This one ___ changed everything for us.
  3. Nobody believed ___ would work.
  4. A year ago I had zero ___. Today, ___.
  5. Let me tell you about the worst ___ I ever had.

03

The 30-day posting plan

Post 4–5 times a week, rotating four content pillars. Consistency beats volume — pick a pace you can keep for 30 days.

TeachBuilds trust

Tips, how-tos and answers to the questions your customers ask most.

ShowBuilds interest

Behind the scenes, your process, a day in the life, how it’s made.

ProveBuilds belief

Results, before-and-afters, reviews, and customer stories (with permission).

PersonalBuilds connection

Your story, your opinions, your team. People buy from people.

04

Phone filming checklist

  • Clean your camera lens (seriously — it’s the #1 fix).
  • Film vertical, 4K at 30fps, using the back camera when you can.
  • Face a window. Light in front of you, never behind.
  • Phone at eye level, about an arm’s length away.
  • Use a cheap clip-on mic — bad audio gets scrolled faster than bad video.
  • Quiet room, TV and fans off.
  • Start talking the moment you hit record. Trim the start later.
  • Film 2–3 takes of the hook. It’s the part that matters most.
  • Grab extra clips of your hands, your work and your space to cut to.
  • Add captions — most people watch with the sound off.

05

The caption formula

1. Re-hook

Repeat or extend the hook in the first line — it shows before “more”.

2. Add value

One or two short lines that add something the video didn’t say.

3. One action

Ask a question, or tell them to save, share, or tap the link.

4. 3–5 hashtags

Specific to your topic and location. Skip the giant generic ones.

Example — a local bakery

Most people store bread the wrong way 🍞
The fridge dries it out in a day. Keep it cut-side down on a board, or freeze slices for up to a month.
Save this for your next loaf — and tell us your favourite bake 👇
#bakery #sourdough #yourcity
