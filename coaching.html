How do I book?

Send a request with the button above. We’ll reply within one business day with times that work and a secure payment link.

Where do the calls happen?

On a video call — you just need a laptop or phone. Every call is recorded so you can watch it again.

Can I cancel coaching?

Yes. Coaching is month to month — cancel anytime and it won’t renew. You keep everything through the end of the month you paid for.

Is this for businesses or creators?

Both. We’ve built the plan around turning attention into customers for businesses, and into a loyal audience and income for creators.

Can I start with a session and move to coaching?

Yes. If you start coaching within 30 days of your strategy session, the $197 comes off your first month.
